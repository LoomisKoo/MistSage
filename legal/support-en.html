Contact us

For problems or suggestions, email magaxy@163.com and include your iOS version and device model. We reply as soon as we can.

What the app does

Post a hesitant decision under "Decisions · Open" to ask for advice, record what you did or skipped and its money and time cost under "Decisions · Settled", browse other people's dilemmas on the "Plaza", and keep your things in "Assets".

Getting started

  1. Set "what one hour is worth to you" once in Settings; entries are then shown as time cost.
  2. Post an open decision, wait out the cooling-off period, then settle it as do or skip.
  3. Log small calls straight to Settled, and note the money you saved.
  4. Register things you own and tap "used once" to see cost per use and whether it sits idle.

FAQ

Why is the plaza empty?

The plaza only shows public posts from your region. Mainland China and other regions are separate partitions and cannot see each other's content.

Do I need to sign in to iCloud?

You can browse the plaza without it, but recording and syncing need iCloud. Data lives in the developer's CloudKit container and does not use your iCloud storage quota.

Not getting notifications?

Allow notifications for MistSage in iOS Settings → Notifications, or jump there from Settings → Notifications in the app. You are notified when a cooling-off period ends or when you receive advice.

How do I block or report someone?

You can report or block from a post, a suggestion, or a user profile. Blocked people disappear from your plaza; manage them in Settings → Blocked.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account removes your account and cloud data. This cannot be undone.
