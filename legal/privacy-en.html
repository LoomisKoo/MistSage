Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

MistSage ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. What we collect

  • Content you enter yourself: decision records, advice requests, advice, assets, nickname, avatar, and time-cost settings.
  • The app identifies you through your iCloud account. It does not access your Apple ID, email address, or phone number.

2. Where it is stored

This data is stored in the developer's Apple CloudKit database so it can sync across your devices. It does not use your iCloud storage. Your device keeps only a local cache.

Data is split into two regions based on your App Store region: mainland China and everywhere else. Users in one region cannot see content posted to the Plaza in the other.

3. Who can see it

  • Advice requests posted to the Plaza, advice you give, your nickname, avatar, gender (if made public), and assets you mark as public are visible to other users.
  • The app provides no way for other users to view your decision records, private assets, or time-cost settings.

4. How we use it

Only to show you your records, statistics, and Plaza content. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

5. Third parties

Cloud storage is provided by Apple CloudKit. We do not share your data with any other third party.

6. Contact

If you have questions, contact the developer at the email address listed on the App Store page.
